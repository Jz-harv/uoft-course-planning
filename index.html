Roadmap

4 years, simply

Year 1

Gateways for 2–3 programs. Keep doors open.

After 4.0 credits

Apply for POSt on ACORN. Keep a Type 1 backup.

Years 2–3

Core 200/300-level. Finish breadth by Year 3.

Year 4

400-level + final checks. Request graduation.

Example — CS Specialist (12.0): Year 1 gate is CSC110Y1 (70%) + CSC111H1 (77%) + calculus, within 12 months — for CMP1 students only.
